# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep this public-interest project accurate, readable and accessible.

## Helpful contributions

- Correct a legal statement using current primary legislation or an official government source.
- Verify that a linked source remains available and supports the statement attached to it.
- Improve naturalness and substantive parity between English and Simplified Chinese.
- Improve keyboard access, contrast, responsive layout or screen-reader clarity.
- Edit for precision, fairness and clarity without weakening or overstating the legal position.

## Please do not submit

- Names or identifying details of employers, managers or workers.
- Private conversations, screenshots, confidential workplace documents or personal phone numbers.
- Unsupported allegations, diagnoses, blacklists or claims that a specific person committed an offence.
- Promotions, petitions, donation requests or user-submission/reporting features.

## Review standard

For legal changes, cite the exact provision and link the primary source. Explain what wording should change and why. Distinguish legal propositions from ethical commentary. When an issue depends on facts or legal interpretation, qualify it and flag it for qualified review rather than presenting it as settled. Translation changes should preserve meaning, qualifications and scope in both languages.

Submit changes through a pull request with a concise summary. Maintainers may ask for a source, a clearer qualification or a translation review before merging. The project welcomes correction and does not represent that its content has been formally reviewed by a lawyer.
